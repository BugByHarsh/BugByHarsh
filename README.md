## 👋 About me

I'm **Harsh Upadhyay**, a Full-Stack Developer and founder of **WebLum Digital**, based in Meerut, India. I build practical, user-focused web products for businesses and early-stage teams.

I enjoy turning ideas into clean interfaces, reliable APIs, and complete products that are easy to use and maintain. I'm currently open to **freelance projects, MVPs, dashboards, SaaS products, and collaborations**.
